# Hacking AI for Historical Research

_A New Media Lab workshop applying AI methods to small collections of primary sources, paired with questions of provenance, citation, ethics, and commercial inference._

Participants apply OCR, transcription cleanup, structured notes, and thematic coding to small collections of primary sources. They pair those methods with questions of provenance, citation, ethics, and commercial inference.

**Date:** March 2025
**Location:** New Media Lab, CUNY Graduate Center
**Facilitator:** Zach Muhlbauer
**Materials:** [zmuhl.net/teaching.html](https://zmuhl.net/teaching.html)

_This workshop predates the CUNY AI Lab's launch in Fall 2025 and is recorded here from the Lab's [links page](https://cuny-ai-lab.github.io/links/). Its slides and worksheets are not archived in this repository; the facilitator's teaching page is the source of record._

## About the CUNY AI Lab

The **CUNY AI Lab** is a faculty and staff-led incubator for experimentation with AI by CUNY researchers across disciplines, strengthening CUNY's position at the leading edge of technological innovation. Located at the CUNY Graduate Center and led by experienced scholars and technologists who have built widely used open publishing platforms, the Lab fosters momentum for AI experimentation while foregrounding ethical practices and values that reinforce CUNY's mission of accessible, equitable, and transformative education.
