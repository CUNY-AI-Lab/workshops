# Using Generative AI for "Uncreative Writing"

_A Teaching and Learning Center workshop that reframes plagiarism, remix, borrowing, and academic integrity through practices of cutting, recombining, patchwriting, and uncreative writing with generative AI._

Practices of cutting and recombining text, patchwriting, and uncreative writing reframe plagiarism, remix, borrowing, and academic integrity with generative AI. Participants consider how these tools might support writing pedagogy without becoming either a catastrophe or a universal remedy.

**Date:** Thursday, November 9, 2023
**Host:** Teaching and Learning Center, CUNY Graduate Center
**Event listing:** [gc.cuny.edu/events/using-generative-ai-uncreative-writing](https://www.gc.cuny.edu/events/using-generative-ai-uncreative-writing)

_This is the earliest workshop on the Lab's [links page](https://cuny-ai-lab.github.io/links/), from the Teaching and Learning Center's 2023–2024 workshop strand that preceded the Critical AI Literacy Institute and the CUNY AI Lab. Its materials are not archived in this repository._

## About the CUNY AI Lab

The **CUNY AI Lab** is a faculty and staff-led incubator for experimentation with AI by CUNY researchers across disciplines, strengthening CUNY's position at the leading edge of technological innovation. Located at the CUNY Graduate Center and led by experienced scholars and technologists who have built widely used open publishing platforms, the Lab fosters momentum for AI experimentation while foregrounding ethical practices and values that reinforce CUNY's mission of accessible, equitable, and transformative education.
