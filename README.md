# CUNY AI Lab Workshops

A collection of workshops, workshop series, and presentations developed and led by the CUNY AI Lab to foster critical AI literacy among faculty, staff, and students across the university system.

## About the CUNY AI Lab

The **CUNY AI Lab** is a faculty and staff-led incubator for experimentation with AI by CUNY researchers across disciplines, strengthening CUNY's position at the leading edge of technological innovation. Located at the CUNY Graduate Center and led by experienced scholars and technologists who have built widely used open publishing platforms, the Lab fosters momentum for AI experimentation while foregrounding ethical practices and values that reinforce CUNY's mission of accessible, equitable, and transformative education.

The Lab grew out of four Graduate Center units that each took up AI in their own way (the Teaching and Learning Center, GC Digital Initiatives, the Mina Rees Library, and the American Social History Project / Center for Media and Learning with its New Media Lab) and out of the Critical AI Literacy Institute, which brought that work under one banner in 2025. Earlier Teaching and Learning Center workshops are archived here alongside the Lab's own.

## Workshop Collection

### Fall 2026

#### [Sandbox Workshop Series](./sandbox-series/)

Three 90-minute sessions in the Graduate Center's New Media Lab that carry one example, the STEM Adventure Games custom model, from system prompt to knowledge collection to skills and tools. Developed by Zach Muhlbauer as a revision of the Spring 2026 three-week series, with participant-facing copy, recaptured interface screenshots, published lesson plans, and a follow-up coworking session on October 29, 2026.

- **Technology:** [Shared deck site](https://cuny-ai-lab.github.io/sandbox-series/) (static HTML, light and dark themes, full workshop copy for each session) with the CUNY AI Lab Sandbox
- **Format:** Three Thursday workshops, 2:30–4:00 p.m., September 17, October 1, and October 15, 2026
- **Sessions:** [Composing System Prompts](./sandbox-series/composing-system-prompts/) · [Curating Knowledge Collections](./sandbox-series/curating-knowledge-collections/) · [Configuring Skills and Tools](./sandbox-series/configuring-skills-tools/)

#### [CUNY AI Lab: Infrastructure, Access, and Outreach](./cail-oct-14/)

A presentation and discussion on how the Lab operates: its guiding principles in practice, the bottom-up origins of the Lab, where its tools run and a fully local pipeline for sensitive material, the CAIL Gateway and the access and allowance system, Agent Studio, Site Studio, and CAIL Pi, and the Sandbox and tools in use across CUNY.

- **Technology:** [Reveal.js slide deck](https://cuny-ai-lab.github.io/cail-oct-14/) with speaker notes, inline SVG diagrams, and an embedded demo
- **Format:** About 25 minutes of slides in four sections and 35 minutes of discussion
- **Originally conducted:** CUNY Graduate Center, October 14, 2026

### Summer 2026

#### [AI Literacy Basics](./ai-literacy-basics/)

A session for the Teach@CUNY Summer Institute that moves from an everyday algorithm to how a large language model predicts text, then runs two Sandbox activities: the same prompt in two models, and the same prompt with a two-sentence system prompt.

- **Technology:** [Custom slide deck](https://cuny-ai-lab.github.io/ai-literacy-basics/) with the CUNY AI Lab Sandbox
- **Format:** Hands-on session with two activities and debriefs
- **Originally conducted:** Teach@CUNY Summer Institute 2026, CUNY Graduate Center, May 26, 2026

### Spring 2026

#### Vibe Coding Workshop Series

##### [Foundations: Coding with Generative AI](./gen-dev-foundations/)

Core concepts for AI-assisted development, including LLM fundamentals, VS Code and command line setup, Git/GitHub workflows, and Gemini CLI installation.

- **Technology:** [Custom slide deck](https://cuny-ai-lab.github.io/gen-dev-foundations/) with live coding demos
- **Format:** 90-minute hands-on workshop (CAIL Spotlight #1)
- **Originally conducted:** CUNY Graduate Center, Spring 2026

##### [Vibe-Coding Prototypes](./vibe-coding-prototypes/)

Building and deploying web applications with AI coding agents. Participants use Gemini CLI to plan, build, customize, and publish a focus timer app to GitHub Pages.

- **Technology:** [Reveal.js slide deck](https://cuny-ai-lab.github.io/vibe-coding-prototypes/) with Gemini CLI
- **Format:** 90-minute hands-on workshop (CAIL Spotlight #2)
- **Originally conducted:** CUNY Graduate Center, March 10, 2026

#### Using the CAIL Sandbox in the Classroom

A three-week series for instructors, developed by Stefano Morello and Zach Muhlbauer, that builds a custom model layer by layer: a system prompt, a knowledge collection, and a skill. Each week contrasts weak, mediocre, and strong examples across composition, history, and literature before a hands-on build.

##### [Composing System Prompts](./system-prompting/)

How to craft effective system prompts to shape AI behavior for classroom use cases and custom models. Three progressive examples across composition, history, and literary analysis, and a five-component drafting framework.

- **Technology:** [Custom slide deck](https://cuny-ai-lab.github.io/system-prompting/) with copy-able templates
- **Format:** 90-minute interactive workshop (Week 1 of 3)
- **Originally conducted:** CUNY AI Lab, March 16, 2026

##### [Curating Knowledge Collections](./knowledge-collections/)

How to build and curate the set of course documents a custom model retrieves from: what a knowledge collection is, how retrieval-augmented generation works, three progressive examples, best practices and pitfalls, and a three-layer build for one assignment.

- **Technology:** [Custom slide deck](https://cuny-ai-lab.github.io/knowledge-collections/) with copy-able templates and screenshot carousels
- **Format:** 90-minute interactive workshop (Week 2 of 3)
- **Originally conducted:** CUNY AI Lab, March 23, 2026

##### [Customizing Skills & Tools](./skills-tools/)

How to write skills, step-by-step procedures a custom model follows for a specific pedagogical move, and how tools differ. Three before-and-after examples (stasis theory, the sourcing heuristic, reading the cinematic frame), a trigger-procedure-format anatomy, and a hands-on draft, attach, and test.

- **Technology:** Custom slide deck ([repository](https://github.com/CUNY-AI-Lab/skills-tools)) with copy-able templates
- **Format:** 90-minute interactive workshop (Week 3 of 3)
- **Originally conducted:** CUNY AI Lab, March 30, 2026

#### [Critical AI Literacy Institute at Brooklyn College](./cali-brooklyn/)

A Teaching and Learning Center presentation for a CALI faculty visit: the Institute's origins and four areas of intervention, two faculty cohorts, the curriculum and mixed-methods research, and the T(h)inkering strand from Hugging Face pilots to the CUNY AI Lab Sandbox.

- **Technology:** [Custom slide deck](https://cuny.is/cali-brooklyn) with synced galleries and step reveals
- **Format:** Presentation with discussion (27 slides)
- **Originally conducted:** Brooklyn College, March 25, 2026

#### [Creative Coding with Generative AI](./creative-coding/)

An introduction to creative coding with AI, bridging the history of computer art (Bell Labs, Vera Molnar) with browser-based generative workflows using the CUNY AI Lab Sandbox.

- **Technology:** [Custom slide deck](https://cuny-ai-lab.github.io/creative-coding) with CUNY AI Lab Sandbox
- **Format:** 90-minute interactive workshop
- **Originally conducted:** Interactive Technology and Pedagogy Certificate Program, February 19, 2026

## Repository Structure

Each workshop follows a consistent archiving structure for easy navigation and maintenance:

```
workshop-name/
├── README.md                    # Workshop overview and description
├── outline.md                   # Detailed workshop plan and agenda
├── resource.md                  # Companion worksheets and activities
├── archive-entry.md             # Archive metadata and materials
├── slides.md                    # Slide-by-slide reference (presentations)
├── slides/                      # Exported presentation materials (if applicable)
└── originals/                   # Source files (.docx, .pptx, .pdf)
```

A workshop series keeps its sessions together in one folder, with a series README and one subfolder per session (see [sandbox-series/](./sandbox-series/)). Presentations without hands-on activities carry a `slides.md` reference in place of `resource.md`. Recent decks are published from their own repositories in the [CUNY-AI-Lab](https://github.com/CUNY-AI-Lab) organization; each entry links to the live slides and source.

All Word documents have been converted to Markdown for better version control, accessibility, and searchability. Original source files are preserved in the `originals/` subdirectory of each workshop.
