## Description

The GC Digital Initiatives orientation for incoming Graduate Center students, built and delivered with the CUNY AI Lab. It introduces digital scholarship at the GC, degree programs and coursework, the GC Digital Scholarship Lab, the CUNY AI Lab, the Graduate Center's AI Guidelines, the CUNY Academic Commons and Manifold, grants, labs and spaces, and what is ahead. The Lab's section presents its mission, what it offers, its four commitments, the Sandbox, and the Fall 2026 Sandbox Workshop Series, Vibe Coding Series, and coworking sessions.
