## The CUNY AI Lab Section

**CUNY AI Lab (CAIL): AI Infrastructure for CUNY, by CUNY.** A faculty- and staff-led initiative that develops and maintains AI tools for teaching, learning, and research. Collaborating units.

**What CAIL Offers:** Tools · Training & Advising · Community · Resources.

**Our Commitments:**
- **Privacy by Design** - built on providers that do not retain or train on user data
- **Transparency** - favoring open-weight models
- **Environmental Accountability** - visibility into the costs of AI use
- **Criticality** - AI literacy over AI dependency

**CUNY AI Lab Sandbox:** a privacy-conscious chat environment for faculty, staff, and students; open-weight models curated for the CUNY community; configure custom models for research and classroom use.
