## About the CUNY AI Lab

The **CUNY AI Lab** is a faculty and staff-led incubator for experimentation with AI by CUNY researchers across disciplines, strengthening CUNY's position at the leading edge of technological innovation. Located at the CUNY Graduate Center and led by experienced scholars and technologists who have built widely used open publishing platforms, the Lab fosters momentum for AI experimentation while foregrounding ethical practices and values that reinforce CUNY's mission of accessible, equitable, and transformative education.

## Overview

The orientation follows GCDI's 2026 outline: Introduction (digital scholarship at the GC, GCDI's approach, what it does), Coursework (degree programs, certificates, sample courses, a data quilt example), Support for Digital Scholarship (the GC Digital Scholarship Lab and Digital Fellows, the CUNY AI Lab, the Graduate Center's AI Guidelines), Platforms for Scholarly Publication and Connection (CUNY Academic Commons and Manifold), Grants (PDIG), Labs and Spaces, and Looking Forward.

The **CUNY AI Lab section** presents "AI infrastructure for CUNY, by CUNY": a faculty- and staff-led initiative that develops and maintains AI tools for teaching, learning, and research, with its collaborating units; what CAIL offers (tools, training and advising, community, resources); its commitments (privacy by design, transparency, environmental accountability, criticality); the Sandbox as a privacy-conscious chat environment with curated open-weight models and custom model configuration; and the Fall 2026 programming: the Sandbox Workshop Series (September 17, October 1, October 15), the Vibe Coding Series (September 22, September 29, October 13), and drop-in coworking sessions (October 29, November 17, December 3), all at the New Media Lab, with a registration QR code. An AI Guidelines slide notes the Graduate Center's draft guidelines published in June 2026 and the 2026–2027 programming around them.
