## Description

A paper on techno-ambivalence in the datafied university. It proposes a ladder of ambivalence from community to infrastructure: the mixed stance that individuals hold toward AI is taken up by communities of practice that treat reasoned adoption and informed refusal as equally legitimate, and is finally built into infrastructure, where the CUNY AI Lab's constraints (zero data retention, open-weight models, visible environmental cost, criticality) make ambivalence durable and collective rather than private.
