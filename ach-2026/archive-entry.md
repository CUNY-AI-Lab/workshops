## Categories

## Description

At ACH 2026 the CUNY AI Lab presented open classroom infrastructure that retains no user data, including the Sandbox, AmigAI, multilingual transcription, and document OCR, and paired those demonstrations with a critical discussion of corporate AI, misleading claims of openness, environmental costs, and the responsibilities of practical work in digital humanities. The five sections move from CUNY's DH context and the Graduate Center's draft AI Guidelines, through the origins and goals of the Critical AI Literacy Institute, to the Sandbox as an experimental learning infrastructure, a classroom study of AmigAI in intermediate Spanish at Queens College, and the Lab's agentic tools (Tools Portal, Agent Studio and Site Studio, a program teaching librarians to build with AI, PDF accessibility, Kale Workbench and Kale Deploy).
