**Format:** Informal presentation (24 slides, four sections) and discussion
**Presenters:** Luke Waltzer, Matthew K. Gold, Stefano Morello, Stephen Zweibel, and Zach Muhlbauer (CUNY AI Lab)

## Categories

- AI Infrastructure
- Institutional Strategy

## Description

A session prepared for a visit by colleagues from CUNY Central and guests. After opening context from Luke Waltzer and Matthew K. Gold, three slide sections present how the Lab operates. Stefano Morello covers infrastructure decisions: the four guiding principles paired with current practices, the bottom-up origins of the Lab in four Graduate Center centers and the Critical AI Literacy Institute, where the cloud tools run, a fully local pipeline for sensitive material, and how the work is sustained and shared within and beyond CUNY. Stephen Zweibel covers the CAIL Gateway, the application and class-access system with its allowances, administration screens and an MCP server for administrators, Agent Studio and Site Studio, and CAIL Pi. Zach Muhlbauer covers the Sandbox, model comparison, course configurations, the AmigAI Spanish template, the Tools portal, teaching tools, and a demo. About 35 minutes were reserved for discussion.
