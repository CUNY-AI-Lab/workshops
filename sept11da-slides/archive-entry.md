## Description

A panel marking the 25th anniversary of September 11 and the afterlife of the archive that CHNM and ASHP built in 2001. Stefano Morello's segment credits the people and institutions who kept the archive alive, scores the four "final challenging tasks" named by Brier and Brown in 2011, and reports what happened when the CUNY AI Lab pulled every item through the archive's API and ran open-weight models locally in the New Media Lab to transcribe handwriting and audio, describe images, read scanned documents, and recover Flash pieces. Four review tiers (file recovery, personally identifiable information, quality, metadata) lead to a fifth: new opportunities for interpretation. The work is framed as an offer to RRCHNM's active preservation and accessibility plan.
