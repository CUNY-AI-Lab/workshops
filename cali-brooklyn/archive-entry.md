## Description

A faculty-visit presentation on the Critical AI Literacy Institute (CALI). It explains CALI's origins and its four areas of intervention, profiles two cohorts of 22 faculty across 12 campuses, describes the three-phase curriculum and mixed-methods research (including over 400 undergraduate survey responses and three early course pilots), and traces the T(h)inkering strand from Hugging Face Chat, Spaces, and the AmigAI Spanish pilot at Hunter College to the self-hosted CUNY AI Lab Sandbox used by Cohort 2. It closes with five Cohort 2 projects, lessons learned, four policy and strategy domains, and a discussion question: how might CALI's frameworks, tools, and methods translate to your context?
