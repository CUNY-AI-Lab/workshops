# Co-Developing with Generative AI

**Companion Worksheet**

*Use this sheet to plan a small project with a command-line agent and to record what you learned about trusting its output.*

## Before You Build

**Prompt engineering basics:** be specific, provide context, iterate.

| Vague | Specific |
|-------|----------|
| "Help me sort some data" | "Create a Python function that sorts a list of dictionaries by the 'date' key" |

**Setup checklist**

- ☐ Terminal (macOS) or PowerShell (Windows) open
- ☐ `ls`, `mkdir`, `cd`, `pwd`, `cd ..` tried
- ☐ Gemini CLI installed (`npm install -g @google/gemini-cli` or `brew install gemini-cli`)
- ☐ `gemini` → Login with Google

## Critical Questions

Before building, note one answer to each:

- **Integrity:** how would you cite AI-assisted work in your context?
- **Bias, environment, labor:** which of these weighs most on your use?
- **Privacy:** what are you willing to share with the tool, and what not?
- **When not to use AI:** which of the five cases applies to your current work?
