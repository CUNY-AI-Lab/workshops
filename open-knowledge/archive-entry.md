**Presenters:** Stefano Morello, Zach Muhlbauer, and Stephen Zweibel (CUNY AI Lab)

## Categories

- Open Education
- AI Literacy
- Scholarly Communication

## Description

A session for Open Knowledge Fellowship participants on how language models work, what they can do now, what AI extraction does to open knowledge, and where AI belongs in OER work. It explains inference, training, and a documented training corpus; why models are rewarded for guessing and how grounding, tools, and agentic loops change that; the pressures on the open bargain from scraping, lost referral traffic, publisher licensing deals, and the limits of licenses; the 5Rs applied to model weights; and public AI. It then shows where AI can help with OER (alt text, OCR, drafts and translations) when a person is accountable, how to judge appropriate use, and six projects built by the presenters and fellows. It closes with informed refusal and informed adoption as equally legitimate choices and five discussion questions.
